Additional Worldbuilding Information

Vòkhá'ábzhá is a primarily isolated language spoken on an island in the Atlantic; somewhere between Greenland, Ireland, and Eastern Canada. This island is called Pkərròntubzhá. Despite Pkərròntubzhá's diminutive landmass, it has many lakes and rivers, which lead to water's importance in Vòkhá'ábzhá.

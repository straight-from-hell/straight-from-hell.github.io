Basic Syntax

Word Order: VSO
Article - Noun - Possessor - Postposition - Adjective
Auxiliary (modality) - Verb - Auxiliary (aspect/evidentiality) - Adverb

Nouns

Nouns have many modifiers to create new nouns or add grammatical information

Derivational Modifiers

ModifierMeaning
-niDiminutive
-(t)ánProfession
-pshóoMaterial

Example: ma'u - plant; ma'uni - seed; ma'uán - gardener; ma'upshóo - wood

Grammatical Modifiers

ModifierMeaning
-dáungDual
-t + last vowelPlural (default)
-uènPlural (secondary)

The secondary form is used if the default plural becomes its own separate noun over time

Gender Modifiers

Most nouns in Vòkhá'ábzhá are not gendered but gender modifiers can add information

ModifierMeaning
-koFeminine
-meMasculine
-tuHealer

Vòkhá'ábzhá has a gender ternary
